<details><summary><b>1. What does EDOS stand for, and where are we based?</b></summary>
<p>Enterprise DevOps Solutions, in Boise, Idaho.</p></details>

<details><summary><b>2. What are the three words behind our product line, and which product goes with each?</b></summary>
<p>See it: Ork Meter. Own it: Ork Meter Enterprise. Run it: Orkestera.</p></details>

<details><summary><b>3. In plain words, what's a token?</b></summary>
<p>The unit AI usage is measured and billed in, roughly a short word of text. Like the electricity meter on a house.</p></details>

<details><summary><b>4. A developer says, "I just want to know what Claude Code is costing me." What do you suggest?</b></summary>
<p>Ork Meter: free to start at orkmeter.com, installs in one line, usage information only.</p></details>

<details><summary><b>5. A CFO says, "I can't tell which team is spending our AI budget." What do you suggest, and what's the next step?</b></summary>
<p>Ork Meter Enterprise. Book the 30-minute walkthrough with a sample workspace; there's no setup on their side.</p></details>

<details><summary><b>6. Does Ork Meter Enterprise stop a team from going over budget?</b></summary>
<p>No. It shows status at 80% and 100% and never interrupts a developer. Say "visibility and ownership", never "control" or "blocks".</p></details>

<details><summary><b>7. What happens at the end of every piece of work in Orkestera?</b></summary>
<p>It lands as a pull request, a proposed change that a person reviews and approves. Nothing goes live silently.</p></details>

<details><summary><b>8. A prospect asks, "How much will this save us?" What do you say?</b></summary>
<p>We publish our test results, including the flat ones. Every team is different, so we don't promise a number. Offer a walkthrough to see what to expect.</p></details>

<details><summary><b>9. Name three consulting services.</b></summary>
<p>Any three of: Release Engineering & CI/CD; Cloud & Infrastructure; Security & Compliance; Observability & Reliability; Architecture & Delivery; Agentic Engineering Enablement. There's also the AI delivery review.</p></details>

<details><summary><b>10. A prospect asks whether we're SOC 2 certified. What do you do?</b></summary>
<p>Don't guess or claim it. "Great question. Let me get you an exact answer from our CTO." Then hand off to Jeremy.</p></details>

<details><summary><b>11. Who do you hand off to, and when?</b></summary>
<p>Jeremy (CTO) or Travis (CEO), as soon as security reviews, contracts, custom pricing, integrations, legal requirements or anything you can't answer from this course comes up.</p></details>
