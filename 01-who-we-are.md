## The short story

**EDOS** (short for *Enterprise DevOps Solutions*) is a small, senior team of engineers
based in **Boise, Idaho**. We build software products and do expert consulting for companies
that use **AI coding agents**: software assistants that write and change code for developers.

Our website is **[edos.io](https://edos.io)**. Say the name as "EE-doss" and the address as
"edos dot I-O". In conversation, just say **EDOS**.

our advantage. When a customer works with EDOS, they work with the people who built the product.

## What we believe (and how to say it)

These are EDOS's working principles. They're straight from our website, and customers love hearing them:

- **Rigor over speed: ship things that work, or don't ship them.**
  *Like a builder who won't hand over the keys until the inspection passes.*
- **A human near the loop.** Automation should take away boring, repetitive work, not human judgment.
  *The AI does the legwork; a person still signs off.*
- **Security is table stakes.** Encryption, least access, two-factor sign-in: the basics, done every time.
  *Like locking the doors on every showing, not just the expensive ones.*
- **No premature complexity.** We add moving parts only when there's a clear reason.
  *Simple systems break less and cost less.*
