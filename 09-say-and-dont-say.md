Our reputation is built on being accurate. One overpromise can lose a customer we'd otherwise
keep for years. Learn these.

## Words and claims

| ✅ Say | ❌ Don't say | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "Visibility and ownership" (for Ork Meter Enterprise) | "Control", "blocks", "enforces", "stops overspending" | It informs and attributes; it never interrupts or blocks a developer |
| "We publish our test results; results vary" | Any savings percentage, or "it will save you X" | Results depend on the team; we don't guarantee savings |
| "Book a walkthrough" / "an engineer will follow up" | "You'll be up and running in minutes", "start now", "this week" | Setup depends on the customer; don't promise timing |
| "Pricing depends on your team; we'll send a proposal" | Any Enterprise, Orkestera or consulting price | Leadership quotes those |
| "Usage information only; conversations stay on the developer's machine" | "We see everything" or "we never store anything" | Be exact about what Ork Meter collects |
| "In active development with design partners" (Orkestera) | "Generally available", or specific release dates | It's early, and that's the honest pitch |
| "Ork Meter" (the product) | "Ork Meter Plus" | That name is retired; you may still see it on older pages |

## Things never to claim

- **Security certifications** (such as "SOC 2") or **features we haven't announced**. If asked: *"Let me get you an exact answer from our CTO."*
- That a product shows "live" data for a whole organization, unless an engineer has confirmed it for that customer.
- Anything about **products not on our websites**. If you hear about something internally, it isn't public until it's on [edos.io](https://edos.io).
- Specific AI provider integrations beyond what our sites list (today: **Claude Code** and **Codex** for Ork Meter).

## Our names, written correctly

- **EDOS** (all capitals) is the company name. **edos.io** is the web address. The full name, **Enterprise DevOps Solutions**, appears where people might confuse us with other companies.
- **Ork Meter** is two words, capital O and M. **Ork Meter Enterprise**, same pattern.
- **Orkestera** is one word.
- The taglines are **See it. Own it. Run it.** (EDOS), **Count every token.** (Ork Meter), **Know what your AI costs.** (Ork Meter Enterprise) and **Autonomy you can audit.** (Orkestera).

## When unsure

**Ask before you say it.** Email info@edos.io or message Jeremy or Travis. A one-day delay costs
nothing; a wrong promise costs trust.
