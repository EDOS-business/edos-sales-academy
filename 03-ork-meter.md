· **Mascot:** Glurp, a cheerful ork wizard who keeps asking *"Where'd me tokens go?"*

## What it is, in one sentence

> **Ork Meter shows developers exactly how much their AI coding agents are using and what it's
> worth, all in one simple dashboard.**

## What it does

- **One dashboard for every agent.** It works with **Claude Code** and **Codex**, the two most popular agents.
- **Usage by day, by AI model and by project**, so a developer can see where their tokens went.
- **Clear money numbers:** what the usage is worth at the AI vendors' published prices, and what
  the developer's own plan actually charges.
- **It trims waste.** Ork Meter's add-on (a *plugin*: a small add-on that plugs into the agent)
  keeps noisy, useless output away from the agent before it reads it. Less junk read means fewer
  tokens used. *Think of it as turning off the lights in empty rooms.*
- **Privacy built in.** Ork Meter collects **usage information only**. The developer's actual
  conversations with the agent stay on their own computer.

## Who it's for

Individual developers and small teams who use Claude Code or Codex and want to know where their
usage goes. It's often the **first EDOS product a company touches**, because a developer can
try it on their own in a few minutes, with no meeting and no purchase order.

## Pricing (exactly as published on edos.io)

- **Metering is free for a limited time.**
- **Plugin optimizations** (the waste-trimming part) are **$20 per seat per month**, free for the first 7 days.
- **No card required** to start, and **no automatic charge**.

Always check [edos.io](https://edos.io) before quoting a price; it's the source of truth.

## How someone gets started

1. Go to [orkmeter.com](https://orkmeter.com).
2. Copy one install line into their computer's terminal (developers do this all the time; it takes seconds).
3. Connect it, and their next session shows up on the dashboard.

You will **not** install it for them. Your job is to get them curious enough to try it, or to
book a call with an engineer.

## The 20-second pitch

> "If your developers use Claude Code or Codex, do you know what they're actually using? Ork Meter
> puts it in one dashboard: by day, by model, by project, with real dollar figures. It also trims
> the junk the agent doesn't need to read. It's free to start, takes a minute to install, and the
> conversations never leave the developer's machine."

## About results and benchmarks

We publish the results of our own tests at [orkmeter.com](https://orkmeter.com) and on
[edos.io](https://edos.io), including the cases where the plugin made little difference.
**Don't quote savings percentages or promise a specific saving.** Results depend on how each
team works. Say: *"We publish our test results, including the flat ones. An engineer can walk
you through what to expect for your team."*
