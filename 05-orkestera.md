**Website:** [orkestera.com](https://orkestera.com) · **Tagline:** *"Autonomy you can audit."*
· **Status:** in active development with **design partners**

## What it is, in one sentence

> **Orkestera is our flagship platform: you give a team of AI agents a goal, they do the work,
> and every result arrives as a proposed change that a person reviews and approves, with a
> complete record of what happened.**

## How it works (the non-technical version)

1. **Describe the goal** in a chat, in plain language: *"Add a customer-feedback page."*
2. **Orkestera breaks it into steps** and hands them to AI agents.
3. **The agents do the work**, checking the quality of each step as they go.
4. **The result lands as a "pull request"**: a proposed change waiting for a human to review.
   Nothing goes live without someone approving it.
5. **Everything is recorded**: which agent did what, which AI model it used, and what it cost.

> **Real estate comparison:** Orkestera is the general contractor. You describe the renovation;
> it schedules the crews, checks each job as it's done, keeps every receipt, and then walks you
> through the finished work before you sign off. You're at the final walkthrough, not
> holding the hammer.

## Why companies want it

- **One record of the work.** Today each AI tool keeps its own logs in its own format. Reconstructing
  what changed and who approved it is painful. Orkestera keeps it in one place.
- **Cost tied to the work.** Spending is recorded against the task that caused it, not as a surprise at
  month's end.
- **Freedom to choose AI models.** Customers can use the AI providers they prefer, with their own accounts.
- **It runs on the customer's own infrastructure.** Their models, their computers, their data.
- **A human approves every change.** Speed from automation, judgment from people.

## Who it's for

Engineering leaders (CTO, VP Engineering, platform or architecture leads) at companies that
want AI agents to do real work but need oversight, records and cost accountability. They're
often in **regulated or security-conscious** industries.

## How a customer gets involved

Orkestera is offered to **design partners**: early customers who use it and help shape what we build
next. Your goal is to identify good candidates and **book a conversation** with Jeremy or Travis.
Don't promise features, dates or prices.

## The 20-second pitch

> "If your teams use AI agents, can you show an auditor exactly what an agent changed and who
> approved it? Orkestera runs teams of agents on your own infrastructure. Every piece of work
> ends as a change a person approves, with a complete record and cost tracking. We're working
> with a small group of design partners now. Would you like to talk to our CTO about being one?"
