Beyond our products, EDOS sells **expertise**. Our engineers come in and fix, build or guide.
Our website puts it this way: *"We wrote the playbooks and carried the pagers."* In other words,
we've been the people on call at 3 a.m. when things broke, and we know how to build systems so that
doesn't happen.

## What we offer, in plain words

| Service (as named on edos.io) | What it means for the customer | Real estate comparison |
|---|---|---|
| **Release Engineering & CI/CD** | Making it routine and safe to ship software updates often | A reliable closing process: every deal, same checklist, no surprises |
| **Cloud & Infrastructure** | Designing and moving systems onto cloud services like Amazon Web Services (AWS), right-sized for the business | Choosing the right property for the business: not too big, not too small, room to grow |
| **Security & Compliance** | Building security in from the start, so audits and laws are satisfied | Inspection and code compliance done during construction, not after |
| **Observability & Reliability** | Dashboards and alerts that show the health of systems before customers notice problems | Smart-home sensors that tell you about a leak before the ceiling caves in |
| **Architecture & Delivery** | Senior guidance on how big systems should be designed and delivered | The architect who makes sure the floor plan works before you pour concrete |
| **Agentic Engineering Enablement** | Helping teams adopt AI agents with quality checks, records and human oversight | Training a new crew and setting up the inspection process so their work passes every time |

There's also an **AI delivery review**: a focused look at how a company is using AI agents today,
with recommendations. It's a natural first step for a cautious buyer.

## What makes EDOS consulting different

- **Senior people only.** Customers get engineers with 20–30 years of experience, not juniors learning on their dime.
- **We leave systems the customer can run without us.** Our goal is a healthy system, not dependency.
- **We build the products too.** Our consultants know AI agents from the inside because we make tools for them.

## How it's sold

Consulting is scoped by **Jeremy and Travis** after a discovery call. Your job: find the pain,
qualify the opportunity (see [Conversations](08-conversations.html)) and **book the call**.
Never quote a rate or a timeline yourself.
