Different people care about different things. Match the message to the person, the way you'd
show a growing family the school district and an investor the rent roll.

## The people you'll meet

| Person | Their title might be… | What keeps them up at night | Lead with |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The developer** | Software engineer, developer, "dev" | "Am I burning through my AI plan? Why is the agent so slow and wasteful?" | **Ork Meter**: free, quick, private |
| **The engineering leader** | CTO, VP Engineering, Head of Platform | "Agents are everywhere. Is the work any good? Can I prove what changed?" | **Orkestera**, **Ork Meter Enterprise**, consulting |
| **The money person** | CFO, Controller, FinOps, Procurement | "The AI bill keeps growing and I can't tell who's spending it." | **Ork Meter Enterprise**: attribution, budgets, records for finance |
| **The IT / security person** | CIO, IT Director, Security lead | "What data leaves our company? Who has access?" | Privacy and security: usage information only, roles, records, self-hosting for Orkestera |
| **The overwhelmed team lead** | Engineering Manager | "We want to use agents properly but nobody here knows how." | **Consulting**: Agentic Engineering Enablement, AI delivery review |

## Signs of a great prospect

- They **already use** Claude Code or Codex, or are rolling them out.
- They have **more than a handful** of developers.
- Someone has **complained about the AI bill**, or can't explain it.
- They're in a field where **records and approvals matter**: finance, healthcare, government, insurance.
- They've said things like *"we're not sure the agents' work is safe to ship."*

## Signs to slow down

- They don't use AI agents and have no plans to. (Note it and follow up later.)
- They want a promise of an exact dollar saving. (We don't promise that. Offer the walkthrough instead.)
- They need a feature or certification we haven't announced. (Don't guess. Bring in Jeremy.)
