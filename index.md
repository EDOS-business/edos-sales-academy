# Welcome to the EDOS Sales Academy

You're here because you're good with people. That's the hard part, and you already have it.
This course gives you the rest: who EDOS is, what we sell, who buys it, and how to talk about
it with confidence, **without needing to be technical**.

If you've sold real estate, you already know most of the job. You listen for what the buyer
actually needs, you match them to the right property, you explain the value in plain words, and
you hand the specialist details (the inspection, the title work) to the experts. Selling EDOS
works the same way. You open the door and build trust; our engineers handle the deep technical
questions.

## The one-sentence version

> **EDOS helps companies get real value from AI coding agents: we show them what the agents
> cost, help them manage it across the business, run teams of agents safely, and bring senior
> engineers to make it all work.**

Our product line follows three easy words, and you'll hear them throughout this course:

- **See it:** *Ork Meter* shows developers what their AI agents are using and costing.
- **Own it:** *Ork Meter Enterprise* gives the whole company budgets, accountability and a record for finance.
- **Run it:** *Orkestera*, our flagship, runs teams of AI agents that deliver finished work for a human to approve.
